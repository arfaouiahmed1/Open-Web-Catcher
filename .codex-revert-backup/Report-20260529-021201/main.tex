\chapter*{Abstract}
This report presents the design, development, and evaluation of a browser-agent evidence-collection artifact for illegal streaming websites. The work was carried out during an internship at Soft Stars in the context of sports media protection for beIN SPORTS. Before the internship, the company already relied on Puppeteer-based crawlers for known website patterns, but those crawlers required continuous maintenance when selectors, popups, iframes, server switches, or stream-detection logic changed.

The report adopts Design Science Research Methodology (DSRM) as its sole methodological frame because the internship produced and evaluated an engineering artifact for a real organizational problem. The internship produced two related artifacts: an n8n landing-agent workflow used to validate browser-agent behavior and support the crawler workflow as a backup, and \textbf{Open Web Catcher (OWC)}, a maintainable reference architecture for traceable browser-agent evidence collection. OWC does not claim to replace the operational company crawler. It formalizes the approach through an orchestrator, specialist agents, browser tools, traces, screenshots, stream evidence, channel/provider enrichment, and reviewable email drafts.

The final architecture organizes evidence collection around specialized agents, browser automation tools, structured outputs, trace storage, screenshot capture, and evaluation metrics. The database described in this report is limited to observability, evaluation, traces, metrics, screenshots, errors, tool calls, and structured outputs; it is not the enterprise production database of the client organization.

\paragraph*{Keywords:} multi-agent system; browser automation; illegal streaming; evidence collection; stream extraction; observability; provider analysis.

\chapter*{R\'esum\'e}
Ce rapport pr\'esente la conception, la r\'ealisation et l'\'evaluation d'un syst\`eme agentique de collecte de preuves sur des sites de streaming ill\'egal. Le travail a \'et\'e effectu\'e durant un stage chez Soft Stars, dans un contexte de protection des droits sportifs pour beIN SPORTS. L'objectif \'etait de rendre l'observation des sites plus tra\c{c}able: classification des pages, extraction des pages d'h\'ebergement et des flux, capture de captures d'\'ecran, conservation des traces et pr\'eparation d'\'el\'ements exploitables pour une revue humaine.

Le projet a d'abord utilis\'e un prototype n8n afin de valider le comportement des agents navigateur sur des sites r\'eels. Les limites observ\'ees pendant cette phase ont ensuite guid\'e Open Web Catcher (OWC), la plateforme compl\`ete organis\'ee autour d'agents sp\'ecialis\'es, d'outils navigateur, d'une persistance des traces et d'une \'evaluation par r\'egression. Ce nom interne refl\`ete l'objectif de capturer des preuves visibles sur le Web ouvert. Le r\'esultat constitue une base technique pour poursuivre l'industrialisation de la collecte de preuves.

\paragraph*{Mots cl\'es:} syst\`eme multi-agent; automatisation navigateur; streaming ill\'egal; collecte de preuves; extraction de flux; observabilit\'e; analyse fournisseur.
